HOW TO PLAY PAC-MAN

  • Navigation: Use Arrow Keys, WASD, or the on-screen D-Pad. Input buffering lets you turn cleanly ahead of corners!
  • Pellets: Small dots are worth 10 pts. Clear all dots to advance to the next level.
  • Power Pellets: Flashing corner orbs are worth 50 pts and turn ghosts blue (Frightened Mode).
  • Eat Ghosts: Chomp blue ghosts for 200, 400, 800, 1600 pts!
  • Bonus Fruit: Cherries appear periodically in the maze for extra points.
  • Tunnels: Move through side tunnels to wrap across the screen instantly.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: P = Pause, M = Toggle Sound, Space = Start / Restart.
